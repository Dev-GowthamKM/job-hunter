What this does

      

        Polls Workable's cross-company search for every one of your role titles, plus Himalayas,
        Jobicy, Arbeitnow, Remotive and 77 company boards. Roughly 12,000 postings. Anything that is
        one of your roles gets stored with a verdict; everything else is counted and dropped.
      

      

        
        
        
        
      

      

        Re-apply filters re-runs the rules over jobs already saved, with no network calls —
        use it after changing a salary floor or an exclusion.
